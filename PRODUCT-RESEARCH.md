## Matching rules

Routine suggestions require an adult path, no concerning safety answer, no relevant health-context restriction, no reported active irritation/allergy/treatment interaction and a completed suitability answer. Face products also depend on skin type. Oily occasional-breakout paths use the 2% face wash; combination paths use the 1% formula. Dry skin prioritises a moisturiser because the 1% cleanser page also warns about dryness. Normal skin gets a moisturising foundation instead of assuming the acid cleanser is suitable.

Pregnancy, breastfeeding/postpartum, medicines/conditions, unclear safety or suitability, severe symptoms, under-18, periods and intimate-care paths do not receive an automatic product list. Hair/scalp treatment options and oral supplements are discussion-only on otherwise eligible paths. No minoxidil, finasteride, oral hormone treatment or automatic deficiency diagnosis is generated.

All routine preferences retain relevant optional recommendations, clearly labelled and excluded from the starting estimate until selected. A simple or affordable preference does not hide these choices. Existing products need not be replaced. The total is a sum of displayed pack prices, not a monthly plan, cart or bundled offer.

## UI and maintenance

Product cards show name, pack size, ingredients, matching reason, caution, price and direct source link. Prices are not fetched live; refresh this dataset before a formal launch. Catalogue metadata is separate in dist/products.js. Product images are linked to the brand CDN with a graceful fallback. The library in dist/products.html allows category filtering.
